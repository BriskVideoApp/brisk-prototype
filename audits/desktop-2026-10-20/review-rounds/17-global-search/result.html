Scope and limits

  • Front-end prototype search, not a live server search service. Production search must enforce access on the server.
  • PDF/document contents, video/audio contents and OCR are not extracted. Media is searched by name and metadata.
  • Everywhere does not crawl every settings field, private profile note, historical Script version or unopened transcript. Other current pages use their rendered visible content; unexpanded or unrendered panels are outside that fallback.
  • Routine mobile checks remain deferred. Keyboard and role checks are bounded interaction checks, not a formal accessibility-compliance claim.

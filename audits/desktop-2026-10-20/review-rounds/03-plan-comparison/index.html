The three recommended changes

PCP01 · S2 · Needs decision

Restore the visible Monthly / Annual choice

Current: At both1440 x1000 and1133 x1000, the frequency group is2px high while its buttons are40px high. A pointer click at Annual’s expected centre hits the Starter card; Monthly remains selected.

Proposed: Give the existing billing-frequency choice a stable, non-collapsing row above the cards. Keep both labels, clear selected state, native pressed semantics and visible keyboard focus. Retain its existing visual treatment rather than invent a new universal segmented component.

Observed defect. The restored row uses a little more vertical space. Preserve readable text and full controls rather than compressing the plans to recover it.

PCP02 · Refinement · Needs decision

Keep the billing choice available while comparing

Current: Long cards already need body scrolling. At1133px, the plan-action buttons become fully visible after scrolling83px. Close stays visible and should remain so. This is a composition refinement, not a claim that those buttons are unreachable.

Proposed: Keep the title, frequency choice and Close visible; scroll the four-card comparison in one deliberate region. Preserve card order, both Monthly and Annual figures, current-plan identity, readable type and existing plan actions. Make this a local comparison layout change within the existing DS dialog.

Design judgement requiring agreement. Pinning the choice leaves slightly less height for cards, but keeps billing context visible at the point of choosing. Alternative: let the full body scroll; simpler, but the choice disappears while comparing lower features.

PCP03 · S3 · Needs decision

Keep the unconfirmed frequency when going Back

Current: Annual selected by keyboard reaches Business confirmation with the correct annual amount. Back reopens the comparison with Monthly selected. The comparison component remounts from the committed subscription frequency.

Proposed: Back returns to the same unconfirmed Monthly / Annual choice within the current comparison session. Close or Cancel ends that session; reopening starts from the current subscription as it does today. Keep the separate confirmation before any plan change.

Directly observed interaction; proposed draft-lifecycle refinement. Retain only the temporary comparison choice, not a new preference or subscription change. Do not change pricing, commitment, entitlements or cancellation meaning.
